N19 Finsbury Park Station - Holborn - Piccadilly Circus - Sloane Square - Clapham Junction

A bus service operated by Arriva London

HIGHBURY GROVE N5
Monday 20 July – Friday 20 November 2026

ROUTES 19/N19 from 08:00, Monday 20 July to 18:00, Friday 20 November 2026 is diverted in both directions due to water main replacement works by Thames Water.

Towards Finsbury Park, buses are diverted from Highbury Corner via Holloway Road, Seven Sisters Road, and Station Place.

Stops between 'Highbury Corner' (F) and 'Finsbury Park Interchange' (AP) will not be served.

Buses towards Islington are diverted via Rock Street, Blackstock Road, Seven Sister's Road, Isledon Road, Tollington Road, and Holloway Road.

Stops between 'Finsbury Park Interchange' (G) and 'Upper Street/Laycock Street' (G) will not be served.

Please allow extra time for your journeys.

Changes to Routes 19 and 38 and New Route 10 from Saturday 14 November.

New Route 10 operates between Newington Green and Battersea Bridge, South Side via Essex Road, Angel, Holborn, Piccadilly Circus, Hyde Park Corner and Sloane Square.

Route 19 will be revised to operate between Finsbury Park Station and Victoria via its present route between Finsbury Park Station and Hyde Park Corner.

Route 38 will be revised to operate between Clapton Pond and Holborn only, please use Routes 10 and 19 between Holborn, Hyde Park Corner and Victoria.

There will be no change to Night Bus routes N19 and N38.
