BEXLEY HIGH STREET, Bexley
Sunday 6 September – Friday 11 December 2026

Routes 132 229 669 and N21 are diverted in both directions via Thanet Road due to Thames Water works until 18:00 on Friday 29 January 2027 (except between Friday 11 December and Monday 04 January).

Buses are not serving the Bexley Library (F and G) and Bexley Station (D and E) stops.

Temporary bus stops will be sited on Thanet Road / Albert Road.
