HIGH ROAD, IG7
Monday 9 – Tuesday 24 November 2026

Route 667 is curtailed from 08:00 on Monday 09 November to 19:00 on Tuesday 24 November due to sewer works.

Towards West Hatch School, the last stop served on the line of route is Murtwell Drive, before diverting via Manor Road and Cross Road to terminate at the bus stop Love Lane on Cross Road.

Buses towards Ilford will start from the Love Lane stop to start serving line of route from stop Limes Avenue.

Stops Linkside to West Hatch School are being missed in both directions.
